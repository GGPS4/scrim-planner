ECAC Rulebook, Fall 2026 ↗
Placement points

    26th and lower: 0 points.

    Format
    Battle Royale Duos (Build), 2 to 4 player roster
    Night
    Thursdays, 4 tournament-settings matches
    Elims
    2 points each in the regular season, 4 points each in playoffs
    Finals
    Top 50 on the regular season leaderboard qualify. Finals are Dec 3 and Dec 10, 2026, with a 1.5× multiplier in week 2
    Ties
    Points, then total eliminations, then average placement
    Proof
    1st place in each game submits the replay code in match chat

    Duo roster

    2 to 4 players
